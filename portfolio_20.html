SFDC Renewals AI Support — C360, O360

An AI-powered conversational assistant paired with a data-driven dashboard, designed to turn fragmented customer insights into one unified, accessible experience for the renewals workflow.

Overview

Renewals teams struggled with delayed access to critical customer data, fragmented across multiple disparate systems. That gap caused workflow inefficiencies, high cognitive overload, and delayed customer response times — ultimately impacting business productivity and revenue opportunities.

Goal

One conversation instead of four dashboards

Transform fragmented customer insights into an intuitive, unified experience by designing an AI-powered conversational assistant integrated with a data-driven dashboard — significantly accelerating information accessibility and streamlining the renewals workflow.

Role

Leading design from concept to delivery

  • Led end-to-end UX design from concept to final delivery
  • Collaborated cross-functionally with PMs, engineering, and business stakeholders to align product strategy
  • Conducted user interviews and synthesized research data into actionable design solutions
  • Designed high-fidelity prototypes and validated usability through iterative testing

Key Impact

Less hunting, more deciding

Time savings

Eliminating the need to search multiple sources for customer information saves users 20% of their valuable time.

Improved user experience

Users receive customer information directly instead of hunting for it — reducing the workflow by at least 7 steps.

Reduced cognitive overload

Consolidating insights into a single view eliminates the need to manage 3+ separate tools.

Enhanced efficiency

Streamlined access to information supports faster response times and improved customer interactions.

Increased productivity

Freed from administrative burdens, users can focus more on revenue-driving activities like growing ARR.

Consolidated access

Users no longer switch between multiple tools to gather customer data, simplifying their day-to-day tasks.

Consistency is the real AI feature

Designing the Renewals AI Support console highlighted the need for systemic consistency. Integrating these insights with the Sales AI Assistant and other customer-facing consoles is what will make the AI experience feel cohesive across the enterprise.

Learning

Prompting is a design material

Empowering users via proactive AI prompting: deep-dive user interviews surfaced latent user needs and let me translate them into actionable, context-aware AI prompt suggestions — bridging the gap between raw AI capabilities and practical user workflows.

Driving a unified AI experience: this project made clear how much systemic consistency matters. Moving forward, integrating these insights with the Sales AI Assistant and other customer-facing consoles will be essential to a seamless, cohesive AI experience across the enterprise.
