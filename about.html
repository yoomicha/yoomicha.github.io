Work Résumé About

Hi, I'm Yoomi — a Senior Product Designer based in Texas with 10+ years leading complex enterprise SaaS and AI-driven experiences end-to-end, at global technology companies including Cisco and Samsung.

I specialize in turning ambiguous product strategy into scalable, data-driven design — through hands-on user research and cross-functional leadership. Along the way, I've held 9 security-related patents and built a track record of aligning complex stakeholder environments to ship high-impact digital products.

What excites me most about design is turning ambiguity into clarity, and finding the version of a solution that works just as well for the engineer building it as it does for the person using it.
